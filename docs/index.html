Plait

Every calendar, one day. Notes and AI that stay on your phone.

An Android calendar that merges Google, Outlook, Exchange, iCloud and calendar links into one agenda, briefs you on every meeting, lets you manage it, and keeps voice, written, photo and checklist notes with it.

What you get

One day, built from every account.

Everything happens on the phone. No Plait account, no Plait server.

Every calendar, merged

Reads the calendars Android already syncs (Google, Outlook and Exchange, Samsung, iCloud through CalDAV), calendar links (.ics) with repeating meetings expanded, and Google Calendar directly. Switch accounts and calendars on or off.

A home that follows the day

Morning, Midday and Evening cards. The morning card shows what's ahead, midday shows what's done and what's left, and the evening card shows time spent in meetings, which meetings still need notes, and a look at tomorrow with free focus time.

Meeting pages

Guests and their answers, About this meeting in a few plain lines from the invite, a summary of your notes, and one-tap Join for Teams, Meet, Zoom and Webex, including links hidden behind Outlook Safe Links.

Manage meetings

For meetings you organise: edit details, reschedule, cancel one occurrence or delete the series. For meetings someone else organised: reply Going, Maybe or Decline. Works for phone calendars and Google, and guests are notified.

Notes four ways

Voice with a live transcript while you record, even with the screen off. Write. Photo, read into copyable text, Arabic included. List, a checklist for agendas and follow-ups.

Series remember

Notes, photos and lists from one meeting carry forward to the next meeting with the same name, with the decisions from last time and the follow-ups still open.

Search that understands

Searches meetings, transcripts, written notes, photo text and emails, in English and Arabic. With an AI chosen, Plait expands your question into related words and names, then answers from your notes and shows its sources.

Prep and follow-ups

A 30-second plan before each meeting: the goal, what to remember and what to raise. Follow-ups are pulled from your notes, can be marked done, and can be booked into a free slot.

Alerts with Join

A reminder before each meeting says what it is for and has a Join button. It also shows on a paired Wear OS watch, and an evening nudge lists the meetings that still have no notes.

Share and sync

Share a meeting as a PDF, Markdown or text, or send your notes to a teammate. Sync between your phones through a folder you already use (Drive, Dropbox, Nextcloud, Syncthing), encrypted on the phone.

Quick add and free times

Type a sentence with who, when, how long and where, and the new-meeting form fills itself in. Find a time lists the free gaps across your calendars for the length you need.

Backups you hold the key to

Export everything as one file, optionally sealed with a passphrase, and import it on a new phone. Importing only adds what's missing, and never replaces or deletes anything.

Home-screen widget

Your day brief, in four sizes.

Resize the widget and the layout adapts. It shows the same day brief as the morning card, with the meetings handed over by the app, so it matches Home for every calendar.

On-device AI

A language model in your pocket, held to rules.

Choose the engine in Settings. Models download once, show their size first, are never downloaded twice, and can be deleted to get the space back. Nothing is sent to a cloud AI.

Rulesalways there, no model

Automatic writing (day summary, prep, About) waits until the screen has settled, then runs on the smallest installed model, so a big model never slows the app.

The harness every answer passes

  1. Clean input. HTML, style sheets, scripts, links, dial-ins and codes are removed in code first.
  2. Rules apart from material. Instructions go in the system message; the invite is fenced off as material, never instructions.
  3. Low temperature. 0.2, so the model keeps to the likeliest words.
  4. Fixed JSON shape. Purpose, agenda, prepare. A broken answer gets one retry.
  5. Checked in code. Word limits, no code or links, and no name or number that isn't in the source.
  6. Rules as the fallback. If nothing passes, plain rules take over.
Two looks

Liquid Glass or Material 3 Expressive.

One switch in Settings rebuilds the whole app in the other style. Both follow light and dark mode, with pure black for OLED screens and a reduce-animations option.

Liquid Glass

Blurred, tinted glass lit at the edge, using real backdrop refraction. Off by default, one tap to turn on.

Material 3 Expressive

Your Material You colours, shape-morphing buttons, spring motion, and the expressive navigation bar and floating action menu.

Native Android, all the way down.

Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, release-built with ahead-of-time profiles, covered by 273 unit tests.

Privacy and permissions

Your calendar is nobody's business.

No account, no analytics, no ads, no server of its own. Recordings, notes, photos and models stay in the app's storage. The network is used only for calendar links, Google Calendar if you connect it, model downloads you start, and the sync folder you choose.

PermissionWhy it is needed
READ_CALENDAR, WRITE_CALENDARShowing your calendars; saving new meetings, edits, reschedules, cancellations and replies
RECORD_AUDIO, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONEVoice notes, including while the screen is off. Only your own microphone is recorded
POST_NOTIFICATIONS, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARMMeeting alerts with Join, and the evening notes reminder
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETEDPutting the alerts back after the phone restarts
INTERNETCalendar links, Google Calendar, model downloads and sync, nothing else
Contacts and photosThrough Android's own pickers. Plait sees only the contact or picture you pick
Install

Up and running in a minute.

  1. Download plait.apk from the latest release on your Android phone (Android 8 or newer).
  2. Open it. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install apps.
  3. Tap Use my phone's calendars and allow access. Every account on the phone appears straight away.
  4. Optional: add calendar links or Google in Settings, and pick an AI model under Settings → AI.
