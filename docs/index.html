A home that follows the day

Morning, Midday and Evening cards. See what's ahead, what's done, then your time in meetings, which ones still need notes, and a look at tomorrow.

Every calendar, merged

Reads what Android already syncs (Google, Outlook and Exchange, Samsung, iCloud through CalDAV), calendar links (.ics) with repeating meetings expanded, and Google Calendar directly.

GoogleOutlookSamsungiCloud.ics

Meeting pages

Guests and their answers, About this meeting in a short paragraph, a summary of your notes, and one-tap Join for Teams, Meet, Zoom and Webex, even behind Outlook Safe Links.

Manage meetings

Edit, reschedule, cancel one occurrence or delete the series. For other people's meetings, reply Going, Maybe or Decline. Guests are notified.

GoingMaybeDecline

Series remember

Notes, photos and lists carry forward to the next meeting with the same name, with last time's decisions and the follow-ups still open.

Notes four ways

Voice with a live transcript, even with the screen off. Write. Photo, read into copyable text, Arabic included. List, a checklist for agendas and follow-ups.

Search that understands

Meetings, transcripts, notes, photo text and emails, in English and Arabic. With an AI chosen, Plait answers from your notes and shows its sources.

Prep and follow-ups

A 30-second plan before each meeting: the goal, what to remember, what to raise. Tasks suggested from your voice and photo notes.

Alerts with Join

A reminder says what the meeting is for and has a Join button. It shows on a paired Wear OS watch, and an evening nudge lists meetings with no notes.

Share and sync

Share as PDF, Markdown or text. Sync between phones through a folder you already use (Drive, Dropbox, Nextcloud, Syncthing), encrypted on the phone.

Quick add and free times

Type "Lunch with Omar Friday 1pm for an hour" and the form fills itself. Find a time lists the free gaps across your calendars.

Backups you hold the key to

Export everything as one file, optionally sealed with a passphrase. Importing only adds what's missing, never replaces or deletes.

AI on your phone, held to rules.

Choose the engine in Settings. Models download once, show their size first, are never downloaded twice, and can be deleted to get the space back. Nothing is sent to a cloud AI.

Rulesalways there, no model

Automatic writing waits until the screen has settled, then runs on the smallest installed model, so a big one never slows the app.

The harness every answer passes

  1. Clean input. HTML, style sheets, scripts, links, dial-ins and codes are removed in code first.
  2. Rules apart from material. Instructions go in the system message; the invite is fenced off as material.
  3. Low temperature. 0.2, so the model keeps to the likeliest words.
  4. Fixed JSON shape. A broken answer gets one retry.
  5. Checked in code. Word limits, no code or links, and no name or number that isn't in the source.
  6. Rules as the fallback. If nothing passes, plain rules take over.

Your calendar is nobody's business.

No account, no analytics, no ads, no server of its own. Recordings, notes, photos and models stay in the app's storage. The network is used only for calendar links, Google Calendar if you connect it, model downloads you start, the sync folder you choose, and a check for new versions on GitHub (at most twice a day, nothing about you is sent, and it can be switched off).

Up and running in a minute.

1

Download plait.apk from the latest release on your Android phone (Android 8 or newer).

plait.apk for 64-bit phones, plait-armv7.apk for older 32-bit ones.
2

Open it. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to install apps.

Settings → Allow from this source
3

Tap Use my phone's calendars and allow access. Every account on the phone appears straight away.

4

Optional: add calendar links or Google in Settings, and pick an AI model under Settings → AI.
