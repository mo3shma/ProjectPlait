Plait privacy policy

Last updated 31 October 2026. Applies to the Plait Android app (app.plait).

Plait is a calendar and meeting-notes app. It has no account, no analytics, no ads and no server of its own. I do not collect, receive or sell any information about you. Everything below happens on your phone unless it says otherwise.

When Plait uses the network

  • Calendar links (.ics) you add: Plait downloads the link you gave it.
  • Google Calendar, only if you connect it: Plait asks Google for permission to read and edit your events (calendar.readonly and calendar.events) and talks to Google's Calendar API directly from your phone. The data goes between your phone and Google. I never see it. Plait's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can disconnect at any time in Plait, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Model downloads you start: Plait downloads AI and speech models from public hosts (Hugging Face and Google). Those hosts see a normal download request, as any browser would send. Once a model is on the phone, it works offline.
  • A sync folder you choose (for example Drive, Dropbox or Nextcloud): Plait writes copies of your notes there, encrypted on the phone with a passphrase only you know. Plait itself talks to no sync server.

The Google Play edition does not check for updates itself. Google Play does that. The edition downloaded from GitHub looks up the latest release on api.github.com when it opens (you can switch this off in Settings) and sends nothing about you.

Permissions

  • Calendar (read and write): show your calendars and save the changes you make.
  • Microphone, and a foreground service while recording: voice notes, including with the screen off. Plait only records when you start a recording, and shows a notification while it does.
  • Notifications and exact alarms: meeting alerts before they start, and the evening notes reminder.
  • Network state and keeping the phone awake: check that you are online before a download, and finish recording and download work when the screen turns off.
  • Run at start-up: put those alerts back after the phone restarts.
  • Internet, and a foreground service for downloads: the network uses listed above, and keeping a model download going when you leave the app.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your notes, recordings and models live in the app's private storage and are not backed up to the cloud by Android. Delete a note inside Plait, or clear the app's storage or uninstall it, and the data is gone. Because I hold no copy, I cannot recover it for you. Data you chose to put in a sync folder or a backup file stays there until you delete it.

Children

Plait is not directed at children under 13 and I do not knowingly collect anything from them.
